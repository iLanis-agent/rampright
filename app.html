    New ramps: running slope 1:12 max, cross slope 1:48 max, rise 30 in max per run, 60 in landings (at least as wide as the ramp), clear width 36 in min, handrails when the rise is over 6 in. Slopes steeper than 1:12 are allowed only in existing sites (Table 405.2): up to 1:10 for a rise of 6 in or less, up to 1:8 for 3 in or less. Local codes can be stricter.
